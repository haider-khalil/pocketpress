Get the PocketPress app

Install PocketPress on your phone or computer and it works like normal software: its own icon, its own window, and it keeps working without internet. It is free, small and updates itself.

You are already using the PocketPress app.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app above. If you don't see it, tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Open PocketPress from your home screen.

Tip: in your Gallery or Files app, tap Share on any photo or PDF and choose PocketPress to compress it straight away.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Apple only allows this from Safari, not Chrome.

Windows and Mac

  1. Open this page in Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
  2. Click Install app above, or the install icon at the right end of the address bar.
  3. PocketPress gets its own window and a desktop or Start menu icon.

After installing, right-click a photo or PDF and choose Open with → PocketPress.

Why install?

  • Works offline. Compress files with no internet connection.
  • One tap away. No searching for the website every time.
  • Still private. Files are processed on your device, exactly like the website.
  • Always up to date. New features arrive automatically, nothing to download again.
