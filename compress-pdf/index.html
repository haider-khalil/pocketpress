How to use it

  1. Add your PDF.
  2. Strong mode gives the biggest savings. Turn on Black & white for scanned forms and ID copies.
  3. Use Light mode instead if you need to keep text selectable.

Questions

Why can't I select text after compressing?

Strong mode redraws each page as an image, which is what makes scans so much smaller. Choose Light mode to keep the text, with smaller savings.

My PDF is password-protected

Remove the password first in the app you normally use to open it, then add it here.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens inside your browser on your own phone or computer. Your files are never sent to a server, so private documents stay private.

Is PocketPress free?

Yes, completely free with no sign-up and no watermark.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It works in Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox on Android, iPhone, Windows and Mac. You can also install it from your browser menu and use it offline.
