How to use it

  1. Add a clear, front-facing photo taken against a plain wall.
  2. Choose the size, move the box around your head and shoulders, and tap Make background white if you need it.
  3. Save, then Download the photo for online forms or tap Print sheet for copies to print.

Questions

Which size should I choose?

35×45 mm is used for Pakistani, UK, European and many other passports, ID cards and forms. 2×2 inch is for US visas and passports. Always follow the instructions of the office you are applying to.

Is the white background done on my phone?

Yes. A small background tool (about 6 MB) is downloaded the first time you use it and runs on your device. Your photo is never uploaded.

How do I print it?

Tap Print sheet to get 8 copies on one 4×6 inch (10×15 cm) photo, or more on A4. Print at 100% size, without fit-to-page, at any photo shop.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens inside your browser on your own phone or computer. Your files are never sent to a server, so private documents stay private.

Is PocketPress free?

Yes, completely free with no sign-up and no watermark.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It works in Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox on Android, iPhone, Windows and Mac. You can also install it from your browser menu and use it offline.
