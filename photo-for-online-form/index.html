FAQ

Questions

What size does my form need?

Every form is different. The quick sizes cover the most common limits, but always read the instructions on your form and change the KB number to match.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens inside your browser on your own phone or computer. Your files are never sent to a server, so private documents stay private.

Is PocketPress free?

Yes, completely free with no sign-up and no watermark.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It works in Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox on Android, iPhone, Windows and Mac. You can also install it from your browser menu and use it offline.
