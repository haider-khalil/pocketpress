Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-08

Your files

Files you add to PocketPress are processed entirely inside your web browser on your own device. They are never uploaded, sent to a server, stored by us, or seen by anyone else. When you close the page, they are gone from PocketPress.

Information we collect

PocketPress has no accounts and no sign-up. We do not collect your name, email address or any personal details.

Our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, for security and reliability. These logs never contain your files.

If we add privacy-friendly visitor statistics in the future, they will count page visits only, without cookies that track you across websites, and this policy will be updated.

Saved preferences

Your language choice is saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it. It never leaves your device and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Advertising

PocketPress currently shows no ads. If ads are added later, the advertising provider may use cookies, and this policy will explain how and how to opt out before that happens.

Children

PocketPress does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Please open an issue on GitHub.
