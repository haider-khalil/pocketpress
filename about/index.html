About PocketPress

PocketPress is a free tool for everyday file problems: a photo that is too big for an online form, a scanned PDF that will not attach to an email, a presentation too heavy for WhatsApp.

Most compressor websites upload your files to their servers. PocketPress does not. Every file is processed inside your own browser, on your own phone or computer, so ID cards, certificates and personal documents never leave your device.

What it can do

  • Compress and resize JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP and iPhone HEIC photos
  • Fit an image under a size limit such as 20, 50, 100 or 200 KB
  • Compress, merge and create PDFs
  • Shrink Word, Excel and PowerPoint files by compressing their pictures
  • Work offline once installed from your browser menu

Open source

The full source code is public on GitHub, so anyone can check that files are never uploaded.
